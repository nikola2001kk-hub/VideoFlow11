VideoFlow11 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Overview

VideoFlow11 is a desktop application for managing video content and interacting with TikTok through TikTok's official APIs.

2. Information We Process

When you connect a TikTok account, the application may receive information provided by TikTok as part of authorization, such as an account identifier and basic profile information permitted by the granted scopes.

3. Access Tokens

Authorization credentials received from TikTok are used to provide the requested TikTok functionality. They are stored locally by the desktop application and are not intentionally sold or shared with third parties.

4. Video Files

Video files selected by the user are processed locally as needed for the application's features and may be transmitted to TikTok when the user requests an upload through TikTok's official APIs.

5. Third Parties

TikTok processes information according to TikTok's own privacy policies and terms. VideoFlow11 does not control TikTok's processing of information after it is transmitted to TikTok.

6. Data Retention

Application settings and connected-account information may remain in the application's local configuration until the user removes them or deletes the application data.

7. Security

Users should protect access to their computer and application configuration. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact the VideoFlow11 application operator using the contact information provided with the application.
